# Market holidays for TA Gyaani — days the NSE equity market is closed.
# One date per line, dd/mm/yyyy (e.g. 14/04/2026). Lines starting with # are
# ignored, so a date may be followed by a # line naming the holiday.
#
# What a listed date does (calculators/market_calendar.py): it is not a trading
# day. The engine does not fetch, the metrics loop does not run, and none of
# the end-of-day jobs runs — archive, cleanup and token renewal alike. It is
# also skipped when the morning step finds "the previous trading day" to file
# yesterday's close under.
#
# Why it matters: a holiday NOT listed here is polled all day and archived as a
# trading day — a false sample in every stock's every 15-minute block, and a
# false daily row in the gap history and beta, with no error anywhere.
#
# UPDATE EACH YEAR, as soon as NSE publishes the next year's trading-holiday
# list (late in the year; it is on nseindia.com): add next year's dates below. A missing file, a
# line that is not a date, or no date for the current year is logged at ERROR
# and the app still runs, skipping weekends plus whatever dates did parse.
